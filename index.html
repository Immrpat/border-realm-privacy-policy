Privacy Policy

For the Border Realm Android game · Effective October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information is stored by Border Realm and what information may be processed by Google’s advertising and consent services. It applies to the Border Realm Android game and its home-screen widget. Border Realm is intended for children and older players.

Age screen and ad choice

On first Android launch, before any advertising or consent SDK is started, the game offers a neutral age screen. You may enter your month, day, and year of birth to check whether optional rewarded ads are available, or continue with ads off. The date is checked on your device and discarded; it is not sent to TinyWidget RPG or Google and is not saved. The app stores only a local ad setting, such as under 18, adult, or ads off. You can review this choice in Settings → Age & ad access.

Players under 18 and players who continue with ads off can use the full game and earn Star Seals through play. When an under-18 or ads-off choice is active at app startup, the game does not initialize or make requests to Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) or Mobile Ads SDK. Turning ads off during an adult session prevents new ad requests; a consent request that already began in that adult session may finish. Optional rewarded ads and Google advertising/consent services are available only after an adult age choice.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your age/ad setting, progress, party and equipment, settings, collected items and currencies, summon history and guarantees, offline progress, and ad-reward receipts in the app’s private storage. The home-screen widget reads a saved game snapshot and stores the local timing information needed for offline progress.

The game does not require an account. TinyWidget RPG does not operate a game server, cloud save, custom analytics service, or in-game payment service. Your saved game data is not sent to a TinyWidget RPG server. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the game removes its locally saved progress.

Advertising and Google services

For profiles with an adult age choice, the Android game uses Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded video ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising privacy choices. Ads are optional: the game can be played without watching them. You choose when to load and watch a rewarded ad. For adult profiles, the game refreshes consent information when it starts, so Google’s privacy services may communicate with your device even if you do not watch an ad.

When these services operate, Google may collect or share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device and advertising identifiers;
  • app and ad interactions, including ad views and taps; and
  • diagnostic and performance information used to operate the services, measure ads, and help detect fraud or abuse.

Google’s handling can depend on your device settings, region, consent choices, and the ad request. Review Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy for details about Google’s services and data practices.

Your advertising privacy choices

For adult profiles, depending on your region and the consent messages configured for the app, UMP may show privacy choices. Open Settings → Ad privacy choices to review available choices, or Settings → Age & ad access to turn ads off. Ads are requested only after you choose to load one and the SDK allows the request. You can also review or reset your Android advertising ID in your device’s system settings.

Google handles information processed by its services under Google’s policies and available privacy controls. Deleting local game data does not delete information already held by Google. See Google’s ad privacy controls for account-level options where available.

Permissions and security

The Android package includes internet and network-state access for the optional Google services and may include the advertising SDK’s advertising-ID permission. The game does not request contacts, camera, microphone, or precise-location permissions. If an under-18 or ads-off choice is saved before the app starts, the advertising and consent SDKs are not initialized and the game makes no ad or consent requests for that session.

Local game data is kept in the app’s private storage. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children’s privacy

The game is intended for children and older players. A date of birth is entered only for the on-device age check described above; the raw date is discarded and never transmitted or saved. The game stores the resulting age/ad setting locally. When an under-18 setting is active at startup, Google’s advertising or consent SDKs are not started and no ads are served. Parents or guardians with questions or a privacy request about a child’s use of the game can contact TinyWidget RPG using the details below. The developer will review and respond to requests as required by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when the current version took effect.
